Four environments

When you want to focus

Set Where to play to Environment and your room fades out behind one of four worlds. The board stays in front of you, and you can move it to a spot that suits you. In the Tunnel, the Sanctum and the Skyline the lights pulse in time with the music.

Tunnel

A corridor of light gates toward a striped sun, pulsing with the beat.

Sanctum

Glass columns ringed with orbiting gems, a Prism at the heart of the ornament.

Skyline

A neon city at night, searchlights sweeping over the roofs.

Aurora

The calmest of the four: slow ribbons of light over far mountains.

How it plays

Easy to pick up

Your eyes and one hand are the controller. The gem you look at lights up, so you always know what a pinch will grab.

  1. Look and pinchLook at a gem and pinch to pick it up.
  2. SwapPinch a neighbour, or drag toward it, to swap the two.
  3. ChainThree or more in a row clear. New gems fall in, and every cascade raises the combo.
  4. Never stuckPause for a few seconds and a hint shows a move. When no move is left, the board reshuffles itself.
Match 4 in a line

Line

Clears its whole row or column.

Match an L, T or +

Bomb

Clears a 3 × 3 square around it, or 5 × 5 from a match of seven or more.

Match 5 in a line

Prism

A turning star. Swap it with any gem to clear every gem of that colour.

Swap two power-ups into each other for a bigger clear: two Lines make a cross, two Bombs a 7 × 7.

Modes

Ten minutes or a whole evening

30

Levels

Each level has a goal: reach a score, collect a colour, break ice or free locked gems. Every tenth level is a boss, with an easier one after a hard stretch. Progress saves as you go.

∞

Endless

No clock and no game over. Pick Relaxed, Classic or Hard, or set up the board yourself, and stop whenever you like.

90s

Timed

A 90-second round for your best score, made for a short break. The clock pauses when you leave the app.

Soundtrack

Real music, not a loop

Ten synthwave tracks, about 56 minutes, so a long session never plays the same tune over and over. The music carries on when you move from your room into an environment. The side panel shows what is playing; tap it to skip a track.
